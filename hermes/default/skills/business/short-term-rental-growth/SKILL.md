# Short-Term Rental Growth

Use this skill when Keith asks about increasing revenue from his Airbnb/short-term rental properties, especially direct bookings, property websites, lead generation, local SEO, or reducing dependency on Airbnb.

## Keith context

- Keith hosts five Airbnb properties in the Philippines.
- He is interested in direct booking / channels outside Airbnb to potentially increase income.
- He is technical and can build/host sites, but wants practical business and deployment guidance.
- Keep advice direct and no-BS: question weak assumptions, avoid hype, and separate proven tactics from speculation.

## Strategy ladder

1. **Inventory the properties**
   - location/area
   - target guests
   - average nightly/monthly rate
   - occupancy pain points
   - best amenities/photos
   - booking rules and minimum stay
2. **Create conversion assets**
   - one landing page per property
   - location-specific pages for SEO
   - photo gallery, amenities, house rules, map/nearby landmarks
   - inquiry CTA: WhatsApp, Messenger, email, or form
   - trust signals: reviews, host identity, cancellation/payment expectations
3. **Lead sources**
   - Facebook groups: accommodation, transient stay, expat, travel, university/work relocation, local tourism
   - Google Business Profile where applicable
   - local SEO pages
   - referrals and repeat guest list
   - Airbnb guest follow-up where platform rules allow
4. **Lead handling**
   - respond only to active requests; avoid spam/mass DMs
   - capture lead source, dates, guest count, budget, property fit, follow-up date
   - use templates but personalize enough to avoid looking automated
5. **Booking operations**
   - avoid double-booking with a calendar/channel-manager plan before scaling direct bookings
   - define deposit/payment/refund policy
   - consider payment rails appropriate for the Philippines and foreign guests
   - document check-in, security deposit, ID requirements, and support process

## Facebook group research workflow

When asked to find Facebook groups:

1. Ask for or infer each property area/city/neighborhood.
2. Search for public group candidates using queries such as:
   - `site:facebook.com/groups "<area> accommodation"`
   - `site:facebook.com/groups "<area> transient"`
   - `site:facebook.com/groups "<area> staycation"`
   - `site:facebook.com/groups "<area> apartment short term"`
   - `site:facebook.com/groups "<area> expats"`
3. Return a shortlist with:
   - group name
   - URL
   - public/private if visible
   - apparent relevance
   - recommended posting/monitoring angle
4. Be explicit that private group scraping is unreliable and may violate rules; prefer manual joining, saved searches, and ethical responses to people actively asking.
5. Draft non-spammy posts/comments that follow group rules and point to a direct booking landing page or inquiry channel.

## Pitfalls

- Facebook is hostile to automation. Do not promise reliable scraping of private groups.
- Do not recommend spammy mass-DM tactics.
- A direct-booking site without trust signals, payment policy, and availability handling may create more support burden than revenue.
- If using the same property across Airbnb and direct booking, solve calendar sync before pushing significant direct traffic.
- For security-sensitive booking/payment flows, prefer reputable payment providers and avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive data.
